bayesflow-xai

featured

Explainable AI for BayesFlow simulation-based inference. Three simulators (SIR epidemic, Lotka-Volterra, Gaussian Random Field) are trained with BayesFlow and explained with four XAI methods: Integrated Gradients, Saliency Maps, Attention Rollout and Masking/Occlusion. Everything plugs into one registry, so adding a new simulator or method takes a single decorator.
